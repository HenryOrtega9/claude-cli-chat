32x8 RGB matrix simulator (TC001 native). Animations run live. Black background = unlit pixels.

idle
walking crab pauses every 7 s and rotates through six vignettes: sleep → dance → visit → stargaze → rest → rain (full tour in ~42 s; bump Speed to 2x to see all of them in 21 s)
thinking
Claude is reasoning or running tools, verbs scroll until done
needs_permission
approval prompt waiting, pulsing alert
complete
turn finished, checkmark stroke then settle
ready
post-complete attentive rest, breathing crab + sentinel scanner
Each frame is 32 wide x 8 tall. thinking / needs_permission / complete: the 13x8 Claude crab sits in the leftmost cell; cols 14-31 carry the state-specific animation. idle: a scaled-down 8x5 crab walks back and forth across the full matrix width. Layered live behaviors: eye blinks (~5 s), jumps into the 3 rows of headroom (~13 s), and pauses (~7 s). Each pause picks the next vignette from a 6-deep rotation: sleep closes the eyes and drifts two dusty-blue Z's up and right; dance puts headphones on the head, bounces the body to a 300 ms beat, taps the legs fast, and emits cream music notes rising from both sides; visit drifts a warm-yellow firefly companion in from the empty side of the matrix, hovers it next to the crab while pulsing brighter, then drifts it up and away; stargaze twinkles five fixed cream stars above the crab with staggered phases and streaks a shooting star across row 0 mid-vignette; rest lowers the body 1 px, tucks the legs, and fades a 4 px warm-brown cushion in and out underneath; rain draws an umbrella above the head (8 px brim, 4 px peak, 1 px handle) while pale-blue drops fall from row 0 through the matrix — drops whose x falls under the umbrella are caught and never appear below row 0. Pause time is subtracted from the walk clock so the crab resumes walking in-phase after every vignette. ready: the attentive post-complete rest state. 13x8 crab centered at cols 10-22, full intensity with a slow blink. Mirror scanner: outward-radiating cream comets (head + 2-pixel fading trail) spawn from the crab's edges every 1.5s and fall off the matrix edges — up to 2 comets per side in flight at any time. Each comet emerges from behind the crab over its first ~500ms. "READY" scrolls right-to-left underneath everything on an 8s cycle. Stays in this state for 90 seconds of inactivity, then drops into idle. Edit screens/preview.html directly to iterate; reload the page to see changes. Animations are pushed to the TC001 via Awtrix Light's /api/custom endpoint; per-frame motion (idle walk, jumps if added) requires a Mac-side animator daemon pushing draw-array updates every ~150-220ms.
